# Herse design

**A notebook is a page.** Herse sets a notebook like a printed paper:
prose in a reading serif, code in a quiet block, figures on the page.
Everything mechanical (counts, run buttons, folds, selection) lives in the
margin, and one colour, dew, marks where you are and what is live.

The logo is a pale grey swash on Herse blue (`#EEEEEE` on `#0682FE`, or the
reverse); the originals are in `brand/`. The app icon is the blue tile, and
inside the app the swash appears alone, in that blue in both themes. The
blue is the brand's: inside the app, dew stays the one accent.

## Principles

1. **Page and margin.** Content gets the page; machinery gets the margin.
   A cell never changes size or position when you select it, edit it or run
   it. Only the margin and the block's outline change.
2. **Paper, ink and dew.** Warm neutrals for everything, and one accent,
   dew (a deep teal), used only for state: the selected cell, the editor,
   focus, the caret, text selection, links, find hits, the live kernel, and
   controls that are on. Other colour is information: syntax, charts, errors.
3. **Reads like a paper.** Markdown is set in Charter, a serif made for
   screens, at a comfortable measure. Code is SF Mono and the interface is
   SF Pro, so you can always tell prose, code and controls apart at a glance.
4. **Direct.** One click puts the cursor where you clicked; a drag selects
   text to copy; everything you can see you can select and copy, errors
   included. Failures stay on screen until you dismiss them.
5. **Keyboard first, shown plainly.** Every surface you reach by key shows
   the key: ⌘-glyph keycaps in the palette and the welcome screen, a hint row
   in overlays.
6. **Quiet until needed.** Run buttons, folds, close buttons and the hover
   bar appear on hover or selection.
7. **One kit.** Every control is built from `crates/app/src/ui.rs`: one
   control height, one radius scale, one icon set at one size.

## Tokens

| Token | Light | Dark | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page | `#FFFFFF` | `#191817` | the notebook card, overlays |
| Desk | `#F7F6F4` | `#141312` | sidebar, tab strip, status line |
| Block | page + 3.5% ink | page + 5.5% ink | code cells, inline code |
| Ink | `#1C1A17` | `#ECEAE6` | text |
| Pencil | `#78736C` | `#9C968F` | secondary text, prompts, icons |
| Hairline | `#E8E6E2` | `#2B2927` | borders |
| **Dew** | `#0B7A75` | `#5CC8BE` | state (see above) |
| Dew tint | `#CFE6E3` | `#2C4C48` | text selection, find hits, toggles |
| Error | `#B3261E` | `#F2867E` | errors, a dead kernel |

The palette is a GPUI Kit theme set, `crates/app/assets/herse-theme.json`,
generated by `crates/app/assets/make_theme.py`; dew is the theme's `primary`
and `ring`. Syntax colours are muted and share one lightness: plum keywords,
blue functions, green strings, copper numbers, ochre types, rose escapes,
italic warm-grey comments.

**Type.**

| Role | Font | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Interface | SF Pro | 13 px; meta 11.5 px |
| Prose (Markdown) | Charter | 15 px; headings in Charter bold |
| Code and plain-text outputs | SF Mono | 12.5 px on a 19 px line |
| Wordmark | Charter bold | 26 px |

**Shape.** Radius 6 px for controls, 8 px for blocks (code cells, outputs,
banners, the find bar), 10 px for the page card and overlays. Hairlines are
1 px. Controls are 24 px tall; icons are Lucide outlines at 14 px. A 4 px
grid.

## Surfaces

- **Window.** One warm desk: the sidebar runs the full height, and the
  notebook is a white page (10 px radius, hairline border) inset 8 px from
  the window edge (Arc, Dia). A 40 px strip at the top of the sidebar and the
  content column is the drag area; the traffic lights sit in the sidebar's.
- **Sidebar.** Two toggles beside the traffic lights: a folder (Files) and a
  table of contents (Outline). Then the folder's name, a quiet button with a
  chevron that opens the folder menu (Open Folder…, recent folders, Reveal in
  Finder, Copy Path, Collapse / Expand All), then the tree: 28 px
  rows, rounded selection inset 8 px from the edges, counts in pencil.
- **Tabs.** Pills in the content strip. The active tab is page-white with a
  hairline, the same paper as the card below; others are pencil. Close shows
  on hover; an unsaved tab shows a dot instead. A single click in the sidebar
  opens a preview tab, its title in italics, which the next single click
  replaces; double-clicking, or editing, keeps it (VS Code, Zed). At the
  strip's right, the
  notebook's commands as quiet icons: Run All, Interrupt (only while
  something runs) and Restart (once a kernel is running).
- **Cells.**
  - The margin holds the prompt (`[12]`, dew while running), the run button
    on hover or selection, and the heading fold.
  - The selected cell has a 3 px bar of dew in the margin, running the full
    height of the cell (source and outputs). Hovering a cell shows the bar in
    faint ink.
  - Code sits in a borderless block. Editing gives the block a dew outline
    and a soft dew ring. The 1 px border is always there, so nothing moves.
  - Markdown sits directly on the page, in Charter, at an 820 px measure
    (~85 characters). Selected, it gets only the bar: no box.
  - Outputs follow the code with no frame of their own. Errors are a red
    tint. All output text is selectable, and every output (text, figure,
    table) shows a Copy chip at its top-right on hover; a DataFrame also has
    an expand chip beside it that opens the table full size.
- **Clicking.** A click on code puts the cursor there (VS Code, Zed); a drag
  selects instead, for copying. Markdown edits on a double click (the cursor
  lands on the word you clicked) or ↩, so its links stay clickable. A click on
  the page around the cells puts the editor away.
- **Cell commands by mouse.** Right-click a cell's margin or source for its
  commands, each with its key (outputs and the editor keep their own menus).
  Hovering a cell shows a + at the foot of its margin, which adds a cell
  below; after the last cell, quiet "+ Code" and "+ Markdown" buttons.
- **Problems.** A kernel that won't start or dies, a failed save: a banner
  at the top of the page with the error as selectable text, Copy, the fix
  (Choose Kernel…) and a close button. It stays until dismissed, or until
  the trouble is over (a kernel starts, a save goes through). Passing
  messages ("Saved", "Copied") go to the status line for a few seconds.
- **Overlays.** The page dims under them (ink at 22 %, 45 % in dark) so
  what's in front reads as in front, and they arrive in 170 ms: a fade, the
  panel sliding the last 10 px down into place (ease-out). 600 px wide, 10 px radius, soft shadow, at a fifth of the
  window height. A 15 px input, 30 px rows, keycaps on the right, a hint row
  at the bottom.
- **The command menu (⌘K).** The pickers' overlay, one list for
  everything: commands (⌘ icon, their keys as keycaps), notebooks (open,
  recent, the folder's), and this notebook's headings (#). Empty, it offers
  open and recent notebooks and the common commands; typing ranks a word
  starting with the query first, commands before notebooks before
  headings. `?` opens it filtered to commands, as the shortcut sheet.
- **Settings (⌘,).** The same overlay, as a list of choices with their
  current value at the right; choosing one applies it and keeps the list
  open, so the change is seen.
- **Share.** A sheet over the workspace, 540 px: the account on one line
  (a dew dot), the choices as check rows, warnings in a pale amber box,
  one primary button (dew), and the link in a quiet well with Copy and
  Open. Comments on the link follow under a small-caps heading.
- **The file tree's icons.** Folders are pencil outlines that open when
  their row does. A notebook is a page with a folded corner holding Herse's
  brackets, in Jupyter's orange softened to the paper: the one hue in the
  tree besides dew. Data files are pencil outlines that say what they hold:
  a sheet (CSV, TSV, Parquet, Arrow, Excel), braces (JSON) or a cylinder
  (SQLite), with their size where a notebook has its cell count. Names are
  shown whole, the extension one step lighter.
- **Tables (a data file, or a DataFrame at full size).** The page holds the
  table edge to edge. A toolbar: the name in semibold with its kind in pencil
  (CSV, `notebook.ipynb · cell 4`), and under it one pencil line of facts
  (1,100,000 rows × 12 columns · 95.9 MB; no timings, only "reading 40%" or
  "sorting…" while it's still going);
  at the right a sheet or table menu, a 240 px search field, Copy and New
  Notebook as quiet menus. While a file is read, a 2 px dew line under the
  toolbar fills. DataFrames with rules, not grids: each column's name in
  semibold with its type in pencil beneath, a strong rule under the header,
  hairline rules between rows, no verticals, numbers right-aligned in tabular
  figures, a pandas index in bold, missing values in pencil (the source's
  `NaN` / `NA`, `null` from typed sources, nothing for an empty CSV field).
  Row numbers sit in pencil in a gutter that, with the index, stays put when
  the table scrolls sideways (a hairline marks its edge once something has
  gone under it). Under each name and type, the column's profile, 52 px, in
  ink tints (no hue): a histogram for numbers and ISO date / time text with
  the smallest and largest value in small pencil at its ends; for text the
  three most common values as bars with their share; booleans as one
  true/false bar; a 3 px track at the foot for the missing share. A bar under
  the pointer darkens and a card names it (range or value, rows, share, "Click
  to filter"); with a filter or search on, the rows in view are drawn in dew
  over the whole. Rows take a faint ink tint under the pointer. Dew is state
  only: the sort arrow, a filter's funnel and its chips, the selected cell
  (dew-tint fill, dew outline, its row faintly tinted), a range of cells (dew
  tint), search hits (dew tint). The table's foot is a 28 px pencil line over
  a hairline: the rows in view at the left ("191 of 2,000 rows", the count in
  ink, Clear as a quiet text button), a range's count, Sum, Mean, Min and Max
  at the right (labels pencil, values ink, tabular). A double-click shows a
  cell's whole value in a popover card under it: the column in semibold, row
  and length in pencil, the value as selectable text, Close and Copy.
  Hovering a column's name tints it, shows its funnel, and opens a popover
  card of its numbers. What the notebook left
  out is a quiet ink-tint band above the table with Load All Rows as the
  primary button; a file that couldn't be read is the red problem banner,
  copyable, with nothing drawn beneath it; what a reader worked around
  (skipped lines, extra fields) is one amber line of selectable text.
- **Notices before problems.** What will go wrong but hasn't yet
  (packages the kernel lacks) is the problem banner in amber with a package
  glyph: the exact command as selectable mono text, the fix as the primary
  button, an alternative beside it. An install streams its last lines into
  the same banner; a failed one turns red with the whole log.
- **Fixes under errors.** A line in a faint dew box under the traceback, a
  lightbulb, one sentence with the code in mono ("`np` is defined in cell
  2."), and one button that does it. Only when Herse is sure what the fix is.
- **The assistant.** The Ask box is the overlay grown for a sentence: 640 px,
  14 px radius, a 17 px input led by dew sparkles, the assistant's name as a
  dew pill at the right, one hint line. Replies are bubbles on the page
  itself, with no card behind them, in a 380 px column at the page's bottom
  right above the status line: yours in dew on the right, the agent's on the
  paper colour with a hairline and a soft shadow on the left, its name in dew
  on the first one after each of yours (`code` on a tint, **bold** in bold),
  its steps as small pills with a dew dot. At the foot, a round reply field,
  or Stop while it works. Its name, minimize and close float above the
  bubbles only while the pointer is over them. A bubble keeps its clicks;
  the gaps between bubbles reach the notebook. The cell it works on wears a
  2 px dew ring and a dew label at its top right for 2.5 s after each touch:
  the reader follows its screen.
- **The text cursor means selectable.** Over any text you can select
  (code, outputs, prose, errors, the assistant's messages) the pointer is the
  I-beam; over controls it's the arrow, over links the hand.
- **Dialogs are Herse's, never the Mac's alert.** A question or a note (Save
  changes?, Move this folder to the Trash?, About, the command-line tool
  installed) is a 420 px sheet in the overlay: an icon in a dew-tinted
  square (or the mark, for About), the title in semibold 14 px, the detail
  in pencil, both selectable; buttons in the Mac's order, what loses
  something at the left in red, then Cancel, then the default in dew.
  Return takes the default, Escape or a click outside cancels. Only the
  system's Open and Save panels stay the Mac's: they're its file browser.
- **Errors are text.** Every failure is selectable and has Copy, and stays
  until dismissed: a red-tinted banner above the page (a kernel that died,
  a notebook that wouldn't open), or a red line with Copy inside a sheet.
  No toasts, no alerts that time out.
- **Quick Look and exported pages.** The same page as the app, in HTML:
  paper and ink from the theme, prose in Charter, code and output in SF
  Mono, prompts in the left margin, DataFrames with rules not grids, and
  both appearances (charts are drawn twice; the page shows the one that
  matches).
- **Changes since the last commit.** The margin's far edge, away from
  the selection bar, carries a 3 px mark: green for a cell added, amber for
  one changed (the convention every editor shares; amber, not blue, so it
  never reads as dew). A removal is a short red dash with a quiet "1 cell
  removed since a1b2c3d ›" that opens in place. A changed cell's old lines
  open under its source as a plain line diff (red out, green in, two lines
  of context), with Hide.
- **Open Link.** One sheet takes whatever was pasted (a notebook's page,
  a raw URL, a session link, a path) and says plainly why it can't open
  one, with Copy. A notebook from the web wears a link glyph and its origin
  (`github.com/owner/repo`) in the status bar, beside the cell count.
- **Live sessions.** A person is their color (the session picks it from
  their name): a pill with their first name under the prompt of the cell
  they're on, and their initial in a dot in the tab strip, after a dew dot
  that means "live". The Share sheet's first section is the session: the
  link in a quiet well with Copy, then the view-only link and End.
- **Comments.** A 320 px column beside the page, a shade off the paper,
  with threads as hairline cards in notebook order: the cell they are
  about (click to go), the quoted text (a dew rule), messages with author
  and time, a reply field, and Resolve. The thread a badge click asked
  for wears a dew border. A cell's open threads show as a dew count under
  its prompt; the tab strip shows the notebook's total.
- **Find.** A floating bar at the page's top right. Every hit has a dew tint,
  and the current hit is solid dew with paper text. A chevron (or `⌥⌘F`)
  opens a replace row under the field, aligned with it. Its actions are quiet
  text buttons, "Replace" and "All", like the "Aa" toggle.
- **Status line.** 28 px, pencil, on the desk under the page.
  - Left: an Edit pill in dew while editing, then the position in the
    notebook (size and open time on hover).
  - Right: passing messages, then the kernel. Its dot is grey when none is
    running, dew when it's live (solid while busy) and red when it died,
    always with a word beside it.
- **Welcome.** The mark (brackets in ink, the drop in dew), the wordmark in
  Charter, one line of purpose, four actions with their keys, then recent
  folders and notebooks.
- **App icon.** The mark on a warm paper tile (`packaging/macos/make_icon.py`).
